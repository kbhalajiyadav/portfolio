## Professional context

During a **Technical Project Management internship with Deep Thought Edu Tech Ventures**,
working at **Kreative Organics**, I focused on chemical trade-data, analysis, and
project-management workflows used by technical and business teams.

## Selected contributions

- Directed a Python-based workflow for normalizing chemical names across international trade records; recommendation documentation reports about a 90% improvement in data accuracy.
- Built Tableau analyses that reduced recurring trade-data analysis time by about 70%.
- Co-developed a Notion project-management framework for new-product-development initiation, tracking, risks, escalation, and completion.
- Connected chemical-domain requirements with data-quality and documentation controls for use by technical and non-specialist business teams.
- Worked across colleagues, partners, vendors, and company leadership while translating analytical work into operational decisions.

## Relevance to later work

The work strengthened four practices that remain central to my research: define
the decision, make the workflow repeatable, document assumptions, and present
the result in a form the next user can evaluate.
