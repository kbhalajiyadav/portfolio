## Intellectual-property status

A provisional patent application has been filed for **Quantitative Thermal Imaging**. The VCU assignment identifies **Bhalaji Yadav Kantepalle and Christina Tang** as Inventors/Creators. Virginia Commonwealth University manages the intellectual property under **VCU Tech # TAN-26-099**.

**Status:** Patent pending · provisional patent application filed in 2026.

## Technology overview

The technology uses thermochromic materials together with visible-light camera imaging and quantitative image analysis. The VCU technology description covers comparison of corresponding thermochromic regions and reporting of temperature differences when the optical response is within defined usable conditions.

The work extends the broader measurement program on responsive materials: convert an observable color response into a quantitative result while retaining explicit validity limits.

## What patent pending means

“Patent pending” indicates that an application has been filed; it does not mean a patent has been examined or granted. Rights in the invention have been assigned to Virginia Commonwealth University.
