## Professional context

As a **Technical Project Manager** at **Kreative Organics**, I worked directly with
executive leadership and cross-functional quality, engineering, and SAP service-provider
teams on organization-wide SAP requalification, customization, and validation in
pharmaceutical-intermediate manufacturing.

## Selected contributions

- Coordinated gap assessment, user requirements, Performance Qualification, impact assessment, corrective-action documentation, and implementation readiness across functions.
- Coordinated with SAP service providers and internal stakeholders during customization, qualification, and rollout activities.
- Maintained traceability between process requirements, test evidence, data-integrity controls, and qualification records.
- Used planning and execution controls that reduced the SAP requalification blackout period from five days to a few hours.
- Applied root-cause analysis and corrective-action methods to technical and operational issues and explained system requirements to technical and non-technical stakeholders.
