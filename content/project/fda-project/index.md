## Professional context

As a **Technical Project Manager** at **Kreative Organics**, I coordinated SAP
requalification and validation work with quality, engineering, senior management,
and external consultants in pharmaceutical-intermediate manufacturing.

## Selected contributions

- Prepared or supported user requirements, Performance Qualification, gap assessment, impact assessment, and corrective-action documentation.
- Maintained traceability between process requirements, test evidence, and data-integrity controls.
- Coordinated cross-functional review of requirements, evidence, and implementation readiness.
- Applied root-cause analysis and corrective-action methods to technical and operational issues.
- Explained system requirements to technical and non-technical stakeholders.

## Relevance to later work

The role established habits that now carry into research: define the method,
record assumptions, preserve traceability, and make the evidence reviewable.
